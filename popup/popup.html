Carrier Comparison Companion

How to use
  1. Open an order page on aimexaustralia.logicsync.app.
  2. Review or edit the parcel dimensions and weight (known SKUs/packs auto-match your stored presets). For multi-product orders, toggle Combine to set one custom box size and combined weight.
  3. Click Compare Rates to fetch live quotes, then click Create Order to open the selected courier portal.
